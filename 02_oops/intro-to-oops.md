with time, understanding and writing code got easier and the programming practices heavily relied on modularity and reusablity 


## OOPs vs Procedural Programming

| Aspect | Procedural Programming | Object-Oriented Programming |
|---|---|---|
| Primary focus | Functions | Objects |
| Data and behavior | Data and functions are generally separate | Data and behavior are combined |
| Execution style | Follows a sequence of operations | Models real-world entities |
| Data protection | Less focused on data protection | Supports encapsulation |
| Maintainability | Can become difficult to maintain as systems grow | Generally easier to maintain and extend |
| Reuse | Reuse mainly through functions | Reuse through classes, inheritance, and composition |
| Best suited for | Smaller, operation-oriented problems | Larger, complex systems |
